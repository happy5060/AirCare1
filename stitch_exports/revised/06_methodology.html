Mathematical Formulation: Sub-Index Algorithm

Piecewise Linear Interpolation conforming to IIT Kanpur & CPCB 2014 Framework

Eq. 2.1-A
Ip = [ (IHI - ILO) / (BHI - BLO) ] × (Cp - BLO) + ILO
AQI = max(I1, I2, I3, ..., In)
Mandatory Validation Criterion: An overall AQI can only be calculated if data is available for at least three pollutants, out of which one must strictly be either PM2.5 or PM10.
Cp: Measured pollutant concentration
Ip: Computed sub-index for pollutant p
BHI, BLO: Breakpoint concentration caps
IHI, ILO: AQI category indices corresponding to B

Computational Pipeline Flow

Sequential mathematical execution stack in data engine

1
Raw Telemetry & IST Normalization

Ingests station-level feeds, standardizing Asian/Kolkata timestamps to UTC ISO-8601 representation.

2
Physical Bound Check & Optical Clamping

Enforces instrument thresholds. Values < 0 µg/m³ flagged as sensor fault; optical scattering baseline calibrated.

3
24-Hour Truncated Arithmetic Mean

Applies CPCB's 16-hour minimum valid data rule (≥ 66.7% daily temporal completeness required per diurnal cycle).

4
Piecewise Linear Sub-Index Interpolation

Locates breakpoint brackets (BLO, BHI) for each valid pollutant and computes individual Ip values.

5
Dominant Pollutant & Final AQI Maxima

Verifies 3-pollutant condition; extracts peak sub-index as final AQI, logging the governing pollutant identifier.

Project Scope Boundaries & Academic Limitations

Academic research demarcation, scientific constraints, and empirical evaluation boundaries

camera Limitation 01: Static Snapshot Notice

Point-in-Time Baseline (Not Live Telemetry)

The live station dataset is a single point-in-time snapshot captured on October 20, 2026 (10:00 AM IST), representing 524 CAAQMS stations across 273 cities. It serves as a benchmark baseline and does not represent a live, streaming socket connection or statutory regulatory bulletin.

timelapse Limitation 02: Temporal Asymmetry

Independent Analytical Regimes (2015–2020 vs 2015–2025)

Historical pollutant mass concentrations cover January 2015 to July 2020, while historical AQI bulletins cover May 2015 to December 2025. They constitute distinct observational corpuses and are evaluated independently rather than artificially stitched together, preventing longitudinal distortion.

tune Limitation 03: Scale Distinction

Sub-Index Values vs Physical Mass Concentrations

Live station snapshot figures are dimensionless CPCB sub-index scores (0–500 scale) rather than physical mass concentrations (µg/m³ or mg/m³). Cleaned fields are explicitly labeled as subindex_min, subindex_max, and subindex_avg to eliminate ambiguity.

calculate Limitation 04: Deterministic Analytics

Strictly Deterministic Rules (Zero AI/ML)

Calculations strictly use deterministic algebraic formulas and descriptive statistical aggregations conforming to CPCB 2014 & IIT Kanpur guidelines. The project deliberately omits opaque deep learning, neural networks, LSTM, or black-box predictive models to retain 100% algebraic reproducibility.
