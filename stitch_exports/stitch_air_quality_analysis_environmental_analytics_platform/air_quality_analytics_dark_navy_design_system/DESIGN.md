# Air Quality Analytics Design System

## Visual Identity
- **Canvas / Background**: Dark Navy (`#0B1329`, `#0F172A`, `#1E293B`)
- **Primary Accent**: Crisp Teal (`#14B8A6`, `#0D9488`)
- **Secondary Accent**: Cyan & Sky Blue (`#38BDF8`)
- **Alert / Warning**: Amber (`#F59E0B`) and Coral (`#EF4444`)
- **Card Surfaces**: Deep slate navy with subtle 1px translucent borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or `#1E293B`)
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk / Inter for modern, high-precision scientific dashboard readability.
- **Data Presentation**: Clean metric tiles, readable time-series line charts, clear AQI category badges (Good, Moderate, Poor, Unhealthy, Severe), and map overlays with demo/simulated indicators.
